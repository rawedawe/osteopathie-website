Osteopathic Treatment in Berlin Prenzlauer Berg

With precise osteopathic mobilizations, tensions, functional restrictions and complaints in the back and spine area can be treated.

  • Acute lower back pain (Lumbago)
  • Diffuse pain in the spine area – cervical, thoracic, lumbar spine, SI joint
  • Tension headaches
  • Sciatica
  • Disc bulge,

With passive stretching of muscles, ligaments, tendons and fascia and direct and indirect manual osteopathic treatment techniques – releasing functional movement restrictions

  • Joints with mobility loss
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Frozen Shoulder (Impingement Syndrome)
  • Strains and blockages
  • Stiff neck

Based on functional and anatomical connections, targeting the right regions – tension reduction with myofascial manual treatment techniques and targeted exercises.

  • Ear noises (Tinnitus)
  • Dizziness and balance disorders
  • Temporomandibular joint disorders (TMJ/TMD)
  • Globus sensation and swallowing difficulties
  • Hoarseness

For unspecific, difficult-to-classify complaints, a multifactorial approach can be beneficial. Complaints overlap and keep each other active.

  • Migraine
  • Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS)
  • Piriformis Syndrome
  • Thoracic Outlet Syndrome
  • Iliotibial Band Syndrome
  • Carpal Tunnel Syndrome
  • Other unclear pain syndromes

Support for inflamed or irritated structures by stimulating circulation and targeted treatment of constrictions:

  • Tennis elbow (Lateral epicondylitis)
  • Golfer's elbow (Medial epicondylitis)
  • Tendon sheath inflammations (Tenosynovitis)
  • Bursitis

After sudden overstretching moments or contusions, local tissue changes occur. Muscle strains or bone bruises can cause complaints long after a traumatic impact.

  • Whiplash (after bicycle or car accident)
  • Ankle sprain (rolling of the foot)
  • Scars and tissue adhesions
  • Contusions
  • Mobility loss due to injuries

Initial Treatment Process approx. 85 min

Medical History (approx. 10 minutes)

At the beginning, we discuss your current complaints and past complaints and pre-existing conditions. Here the clinical picture is already narrowed down through differential diagnosis. The examination then adapts to the differential diagnosis of the complaint.

↓

Examination (approx. 15 minutes)

With orthopedic and osteopathic examination techniques, complaints are ruled out and further narrowed down until the most logical explanation has been worked out. Based on this explanation, the treatment techniques and exercises are adapted.

↓

Explanation (approx. 5 minutes)

You receive an understandable explanation with visualization through anatomy software, the further treatment process.

↓

Treatment (approx. 25 minutes)

The osteopathic treatment is individually tailored to you. Mobilization, stretching, trigger points, manipulation, relaxation, breathing and many other techniques are adapted to your complaint.

↓

Exercises & Prognosis (approx. 10 minutes)

You receive an assessment of the course of the complaint and individual exercises that are specifically tailored to your condition.

↓

Billing & Documentation (approx. 5 minutes)

Finally, billing and documentation are completed.

Reimbursement and Payment

Many statutory health insurance companies cover part of the costs of osteopathic treatments. The subsidies range from €30 to €60 per session depending on the insurance company.

  • A private doctor's prescription is usually required
  • You receive an invoice from me which is submitted to your insurance company together with the private prescription
Open Insurance List

Private health insurance companies usually cover the costs fully or partially. Billing is according to the Fee Schedule for Alternative Practitioners (GebüH).

  • Often no doctor's prescription is required
  • Billing is according to individual tariff benefits

Many private supplementary insurances for alternative practitioner services reimburse 60%–100% of treatment costs, depending on the tariff.

  • It is important to check the tariff benefits (alternative practitioner / osteopathy)
  • Usually no prescription required
  • Some cost between €20-30 per month

Through the Health Ticket, employers can subsidize osteopathic treatments with tax benefits. Employees receive a voucher or cost coverage for treatments in my practice.

  • Payment with health ticket is possible here
  • Simply bring your ticket or voucher number to your appointment

For students, trainees, retirees and people with low income, I offer reduced rates upon request.

  • Initial treatment with reduction is €80
  • Follow-up treatment with reduction is €70
  • Select appointment online

No one should have to forgo treatment for financial reasons. In special situations, treatments on a donation basis are possible. The recommendation is what your health insurance would cover, and you can give more if you wish.

  • Book appointment online

Payment is made directly after the treatment by card (all cards) or cash. Invoice payment is also possible upon request.

  • An initial treatment takes 85 minutes and costs €100
  • A follow-up treatment takes 50 minutes and costs €90

You will receive your invoice in any case as a PDF or on request in paper form.
